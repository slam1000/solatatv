Easy step-by-step guide to set up SOLARA TV on your devices

Installation Guides by Device

📺 Amazon Fire Stick / Fire TV

  1. Go to the Amazon App Store on your Fire Stick
  2. Search for "IPTV Smarters Pro" or "TiviMate"
  3. Install the app on your device
  4. Open the app and select "Login with Xtream Codes API"
  5. Enter your SOLARA TV credentials (Username, Password, Server URL)
  6. Start streaming SOLARA TV!

📱 Android TV / Google TV

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android TV
  2. Search for "IPTV Smarters Pro"
  3. Install and open the app
  4. Select "Login with Xtream Codes API"
  5. Enter your SOLARA TV credentials
  6. Enjoy SOLARA TV on your Android TV!

📱 iOS / iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the Apple App Store
  2. Search for "IPTV Smarters Pro" or "GSE IPTV"
  3. Install and open the app
  4. Add your SOLARA TV playlist using Xtream Codes or M3U URL
  5. Enter your SOLARA TV credentials
  6. Start watching SOLARA TV on your iOS device!

🖥️ Windows / Mac

  1. Download and install VLC Media Player (free)
  2. Open VLC and go to Media > Open Network Stream
  3. Enter your SOLARA TV M3U URL (provided via WhatsApp)
  4. Click Play and enjoy SOLARA TV!
  5. Alternative: Download IPTV Smarters Pro for Windows

📺 Samsung / LG Smart TV

  1. Go to the Smart TV app store (Samsung Smart Hub or LG Content Store)
  2. Search for "Smart IPTV" or "IPTV Smarters"
  3. Install the app
  4. Enter your SOLARA TV playlist URL or Xtream Codes
  5. Start streaming SOLARA TV in 4K!
