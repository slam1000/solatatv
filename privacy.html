SOLARA TV Privacy Policy

GDPR Compliant • Your Data is Safe With SOLARA TV

1. Data Collection

SOLARA TV collects information you provide when contacting us via WhatsApp or email, including name, phone number, email address, and IP address for service delivery and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

SOLARA TV uses your personal data to provide and maintain our IPTV service, process payments, communicate with you about your account, and improve our services.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers located within the European Union. SOLARA TV implements industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, regular security audits, and restricted access to personal data.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, European customers have the right to access, correct, and delete their personal data. Contact SOLARA TV via WhatsApp or email to exercise these rights.

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format

5. Third-Party Sharing

SOLARA TV does not sell or share your personal data with third parties except as required to provide our service (payment processors) or as required by law.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. SOLARA TV does not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

SOLARA TV service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.
